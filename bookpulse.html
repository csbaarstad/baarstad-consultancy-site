The problem

Class novel studies are still run on photocopied packets. Print 150, hand them out, lose half by Friday, grade the survivors over a weekend, repeat. The teacher has no idea who actually read until the answers come back. The student has no idea where the rest of the class is.

The approach

BookPulse replaces the packet with an interactive, chapter-by-chapter reading companion. Twenty classroom-ready texts in the catalog: 1984, Fahrenheit 451, Frankenstein, Holes, Of Mice and Men, Romeo and Juliet, The Giver, The Hobbit, To Kill a Mockingbird, Wonder, and more. Each chapter mixes 14 task types so engagement holds across an entire novel.

  • Students join with a 6-character code. Name + simple password. No email.
  • Edition-aware page mapping. Hardcover, paperback, illustrated, the class follows along together.
  • Public-domain texts run in immersive mode (text in the app). Copyrighted works run in companion mode (real book in hand, page refs auto-mapped).
  • 14 task types: short answer, written response, annotate, drag-and-drop, quick check, self-assessment, debate, quote analysis, prediction, comparison, cause-and-effect, text evidence, character motivation, theme connection.
  • Real-time teacher dashboard. Responses organized by chapter, sortable by Bloom's level and standards.
  • AI feedback as a paid upgrade. Optional and teacher-controlled. The free tier is fully functional without it.
  • FERPA-compliant by design.

Pricing: free preview of every text (first 3-4 chapters). $9/mo or $69/yr for Basic. $14.99/mo or $149/yr for Plus.

Why it matters

Reading a whole novel as a class is one of the things that's gotten harder, not easier, in the last decade. BookPulse makes the joint march through a book feel inevitable instead of impossible. The teacher sees the whole room at once. The student gets a task that asks them to actually read the page in front of them. The product owes its design to fifteen years of standing at the front of an ELA classroom.
