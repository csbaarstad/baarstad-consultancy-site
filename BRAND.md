# Baarstad Consultancy — Brand Brief

Source of truth for the BCS brand. Every line of the site, every OG image,
every cold email gets built against this doc. Drafted 2026-05-31. Locked: _pending Chris sign-off._

---

## The thesis (one line)

**I'm the team you didn't hire.** I know the work, and I'm fluent in AI.
That combination is the product.

The moat is judgment, not code. Fifteen years of real domain depth in
operations, HR, and education innovation is what lets me point AI at the
right problem and tell whether the output is any good. The breadth isn't a
list of skills I'm claiming. It's the evidence that the engine works across
whatever shape the work takes.

## The pitch (short)

Plenty of people can build with AI now. Almost none of them understand a
classroom, an HR desk, or a national event floor. I understand the work, and
I'm fluent in the tool. So I can facilitate almost anything an organization
needs and can't staff for: software, professional development, design,
systems. Not because I'm a world-class engineer, but because I know the work
and I know how to direct AI at it.

## The referral sentence (what a warm contact says about you)

> "Chris is who you bring in when you need something built or taught and you
> don't have a team for it. He knows the work and he ships it fast with AI."

If a stranger can repeat that sentence after one look at the site, the brand
is working.

## Who it's for

No single vertical. Breadth is the brand, so we don't narrow to one buyer.
We lead with proof *across* verticals and let the range do the selling. The
common thread in every buyer: an organization that has a real problem and no
internal team to solve it.

- Schools and districts (K-12, EdTech buyers)
- Small and mid-size operators (multi-unit businesses, family operations)
- Larger orgs reached through partners (e.g. Fortune 50 via COAST Tactical)

## The proof (three shapes the work takes)

These are not three audiences. They're three *shapes* of deliverable, each
anchored by one named, real client. Lead with proof, always.

1. **AI Professional Development** — proof: AI training for Johnson & Johnson,
   delivered 2025 via COAST Tactical.
2. **AI-Powered Software** — proof: BookPulse, live reading platform for grades
   4 to 12 at readbookpulse.com.
3. **Bespoke AI-Built Software** — proof: the multi-tenant Discord platform
   running across Valhallan Esports.

Everything else (TpT, Awards by Rubi, MUD, Last Bell, event ops, people ops)
lives in a quieter "Also Built" strip. That breadth is proof the method
repeats, not a menu.

## Voice and tone

- **First person, always.** "I" and "Chris," never "BCS does X." Chris is the
  brand. BCS is the legal entity (Baarstad Consulting Services, a California
  S-Corp), mentioned once in the footer.
- **Calm and muted.** No yelling. No hype. The wife's note ("colors yelling,
  text too big") is a permanent guardrail.
- **Proof over promise.** Show the named client, then the claim. Never a
  capabilities slide.
- **Disarmingly honest.** The "not because I'm a world-class engineer" beat
  belongs on the site. Owning the limit is the differentiator.

## Words we use

facilitate · curate · ship · build · the team you didn't hire · operator ·
know the work · fluent in AI · proof · delivered · named clients · judgment

## Words we don't

consultant (as the headline identity) · AI specialist · expert · guru ·
ninja · world-class · cutting-edge · revolutionary · seamless · leverage
synergies · capabilities · solutions provider

**No em-dashes anywhere.** Use commas, periods, or parentheses. This is a
hard rule across everything Chris ships.

## Visual identity

Already settled, do not re-churn:

- **Palette:** cream paper (`#f2efe9`), deep ink-teal (`#16242b`), bronze
  accent (`#b08d57`), sage (`#3f5d54`). Muted, earthen, editorial.
- **Type:** Archivo Black (display), Inter Tight (body), Fraunces italic
  (accents). JetBrains Mono retired from the running text.
- **Feel:** editorial workshop. Calm, confident, print-magazine restraint.

Pending: a custom BCS monogram/letterform for nav, favicon, and OG image
(currently stock). Should feel like a maker's mark, not a tech logo.

## Disclosure rules

- **Valhallan Esports** can be named as a client. The Discord platform is
  attributed to Valhallan by name.
- **VHEL, XPL, Arena League, ESC, the LAN championship** stay generic:
  "their league system," "the national championship," "multi-tenant
  tournament operator." Trademarked event names off the public site.
- **Johnson & Johnson** is named (public, delivered).
- **JamJam Tea** named generically only: "a multi-unit boba operator in
  Orange County." Never "Jam Jam" or jamjamtea.com publicly.
- **Awards by Rubi** named (the family workshop the voice came from).
- **CommandCenter** is intentionally NOT on the public site.

## What the brand is not

- Not an AI-specialist boutique. AI is the leverage, not the headline.
- Not a one-vertical EdTech shop. Schools is one lane, not the whole brand.
- Not a faceless agency. It's one operator with range and judgment.
- Not a cold-traffic funnel. This is a warm-intro engine: the "yes, look me
  up" link you paste in a DM without flinching.
