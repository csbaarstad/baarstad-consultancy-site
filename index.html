▸ Behind the practice

A short bio.

Ops · BizDev · EdTech · AI
Yorba Linda, California

Five disciplines, one workshop. Operations, people, education, software — and a literal workbench. Built across a fifteen-year arc that started in an ELA classroom and kept widening.

From teacher to Director of Innovation for three years, shipping EdTech at scale. The last six years are the headline: business development, event management, and tournament operations at the national level, closing partner deals, locking venues, and putting national LANs on the floor.

Now: ongoing engagements in parallel, plus the slower builds on the bench. National esports operations. Embedded HR and a custom LMS for a multi-unit operator in Orange County. EdTech I own and ship. And, when I'm not at a screen, hours at the family awards-and-woodwork shop in Yorba Linda — engraving, custom awards, the literal workshop the brand is named after.
