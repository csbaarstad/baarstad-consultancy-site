▸ How we think about it

Most AI training stops at the bottom of the ladder.

Teachers can already ask a chatbot for a worksheet. That helps for a week. The teachers who get real time back are the ones who climb higher, directing AI to fit their own classroom and building tools they reuse all year. Here is the climb, and where the work actually pays off.

Where most people are today
1
Search Replacement
Using AI like a faster Google. A quick answer, a quick explanation. Almost everyone starts here.
2
Generator
Asking for a finished thing and taking the draft. A rubric, a quiz, an email. Useful, but generic, and you start over next week.
Where we can take you next
3
Director
Steering AI with your standards, your students, and your voice, then editing until it fits. You stay the author.

4
Super-User
Building a reusable assistant that does your recurring work, in your format, once, for the whole year.
See it work →
5
Builder
Turning a recurring pain into a tool your whole team uses. For most teachers, this is the one we build with you.

Most people never leave the bottom two. Everything that gives you real time back is in the climb above them.
